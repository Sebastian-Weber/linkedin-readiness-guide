How to get going

  1. Expand a section — click any section header below (like "Profile Picture") to reveal its checklist items. The first one is already open for you.
  2. Check things off — click an item to mark it done. Your progress saves automatically in this browser, no account needed.
  3. Follow the tags — ✦ Must-have matters most, ◈ Nice-to-have adds polish, ↻ Ongoing habit needs regular upkeep, and 💡 Pro tip gives bonus context.
  4. Watch your score — the progress bar above and the Wheel of Readiness below update live as you complete tasks.
  5. Start fresh anytime — use the Reset button to clear all progress.
